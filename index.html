Quick User Guide

Standard operating workflow for Sherubtse College laboratory technicians and managers

01

Select Laboratory Scope

Choose a laboratory from the selector at the top right. Select “All Laboratories — Overview” for the institutional dashboard.

02

Register Inventory Items

Open Inventory to add items. Enter the correct laboratory, item name, category, unit, quantity, reorder level, and storage location.

03

Log Stock Movements

Use Stock Transactions to record every receipt (Stock In) and issue (Stock Out). The inventory balance updates automatically.

04

Manage Requisitions

Use Requisitions to record purchase or stock requests and update their status directly in the register as approvals progress.

05

Track Equipment & Calibration

Use Equipment & Maintenance to record asset condition, servicing history, and upcoming calibration due dates.

06

Export Regular Backups

Export CSV reports regularly from Reports & Export to maintain secure archival backups in Microsoft Excel.

Important prototype limitation: This version saves data locally in the current browser on this computer (sherubtseInventoryDB). It does not yet provide multi-user cloud synchronization or role-based authentication. Before college-wide deployment, connect it to a central database such as Supabase and configure technician and Assistant Manager permissions.
